The craft

Cut from
better cloth.

Every piece begins with the fabric. We source heavyweight cottons from Portugal, merino from Italy, and Japanese selvedge denim from mills that have been weaving for generations.

Then we cut, sew, and finish in small runs — because the difference between a garment that lasts a season and one that lasts a decade comes down to the details nobody sees.

  • 01 Responsibly sourced natural fibres, no synthetics.
  • 02 Made in small ateliers across Portugal & Italy.
  • 03 Lifetime repair programme on every garment.
